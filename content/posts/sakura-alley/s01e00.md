A little while ago someone gave me a "book nook" model of a small alleyway with a japanese style. I have a couple of them - a Sherlock Holmes study and a view of Barad Dhur. In this one, called Sakura Alley, there's a small noodle bar, a couple of empty stools, some cherry blossom, some crates of bottles sitting to the side. Cunningly, at the back, there's a small mirror which means, when you peer into the alley it seems to continue beyond the back of the bookcase. The alleyway is sitting on the bookshelf on our landing, the light twinkling and swaying a little as you walk past.

Every time I look at it, my mind wanders off into speculation - what's further down the alley? who owns the noodle bar? why is it deserted with all the lights on? After a while my imagination placed a figure, seated on one of the stools at the bar, back towards me. What's the story behind the figure? Who is he? Why is he there, alone?

Time for a solo RPG excursion I think!!

## Choosing a system
I've been using [Fate Condensed](https://fate-srd.com/fate-condensed) when doing short solo play sessions - it's easy to roll up a character very quickly. However, there are problems which boil down to:

- The Fate Point economy is hard to keep running. I still haven't found a good way to introduce compels without it feeling a bit contrived.
- I always end up wanting something a bit more crunchy. This is probably a consequence of the sorts of stories I like or my own gaming tastes but I end up wanting more numbers to add up!

I've had an eye on Genesys RPG for a while and since it's a generic system I thought I'd investigate.
